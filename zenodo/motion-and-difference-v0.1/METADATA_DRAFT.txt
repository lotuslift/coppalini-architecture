Description / abstract:
We construct one explicit mathematical realization in which a two-dimensional carrier supports complex phase, square symmetry, projective arithmetic reflection, Fibonacci refinement, and a Lorentzian SO(2) gauge field. The elementary factorization of the Fibonacci matrix turns alternating projective involutions into the exact matrix record of the Fibonacci sequence. The same construction places compatible arithmetic, hyperbolic, gauge, holonomy, and proper-time structures in one bounded realization while preserving their type boundaries. In the Lorentzian gauge sector, a nonnegative gluing functional detects exact compatibility of local bundle data, while an explicit source-free Maxwell family retains nonzero curvature, independently variable holonomy, and unequal proper time. The result is a compatibility theorem: the declared structures coexist exactly without identifying quantities that occupy different mathematical offices.
