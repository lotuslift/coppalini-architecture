MOTION AND DIFFERENCE - ZENODO UPLOAD SEQUENCE

1. Create a new Zenodo upload.
2. Upload 012_MOTION_AND_DIFFERENCE_2026-07-17.pdf.
3. Fill the form from METADATA_DRAFT.txt.
4. Choose the license explicitly before publishing.
5. Recommended: while still in draft, click "Get a DOI now!" to reserve the DOI.
6. Copy the reserved DOI exactly.
7. Use that DOI to generate the CCLQG citation-only revision with:

   python scripts/apply_motion_difference_doi.py 10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX

8. Verify that the source diff changes only the Motion and Difference bibliography entry.
9. Compile CCLQG v1.1.1 and verify that only page 7 changes visually.
10. Publish the Zenodo record. The reserved DOI is registered on publication.

Do not delete the Zenodo draft after reserving the DOI unless you intend to abandon that DOI.
