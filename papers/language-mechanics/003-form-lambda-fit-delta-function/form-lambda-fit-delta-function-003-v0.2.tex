\begin{lawbox}
\textbf{Abstract.}
This paper gives a calculable grammar for constructing, transporting, and auditing formal relations. A construction declares a carrier, admissible transports, a read-only office, an entry condition, a continuation constraint, and an append-only receipt space. Form states what structure is present. Fit is a witnessed compatibility relation; when two independently typed constructions are transported into relation, an adapter earns Fit only by preserving the required diagrams. Function is the operation carried through admitted Fit. Reader return is separated from complete-state identity, and both are separated from retained occurrence. A nine-gate audit fixes the order in which a claim may earn status. Three finite or exact mathematical realizations demonstrate that the method is calculable without transferring certification across resemblance: a real $2\times2$ matrix witness, a continuous-to-periodic return reader, and a representation-difference calculus. Every result remains inside its declared codomain.
\end{lawbox}

\section{Position}

This document declares a local operational method. Its governing question is:

\begin{lawbox}
\centering
\textbf{Can this declared relation continue through difference without deleting what its continuation requires?}
\end{lawbox}

The method does not identify different objects because their formulas resemble one another. It asks what has been typed, what is preserved, what is lost, and what evidence warrants the next use.

\section{Kernel data}

A local construction is typed as
\[
\mathcal K=(X,\mathcal A,\rho,C,\mathcal Q,\mathcal R),
\]
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $X$ is the declared state space;
\item $\mathcal A$ is the declared class of admissible transports $T:X\to X$;
\item $\rho:X\to Y$ is the declared read-only office;
\item $C$ is the condition required before a move is attempted;
\item $\mathcal Q$ is the constraint every admitted continuation must preserve;
\item $\mathcal R$ is the append-only receipt space.
\end{itemize}

The symbols $\mathcal Q$ and $\mathcal R$ are reserved here for the constraint and receipt offices so that the concrete matrices $Q$ and $H$ used later cannot be mistaken for them.

No symbol inherits an office from resemblance. Every equality is read inside its declared codomain.

For a proposed transport $T$ at $x$,
\[
\Adm(T,x)
\Longleftrightarrow
C(x)\wedge T\in\mathcal A\wedge \mathcal Q(x,T(x)).
\]
The gate is
\[
\Gate(T,x)=
\begin{cases}
\mathsf{can}, & \Adm(T,x),\\
\varnothing, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Refusal is a typed result of this jurisdiction. It is not scalar zero, approximation, analogy, or persuasion.

\section{Form $\Lambda$ Fit $\Delta$ Function}

The expression
\[
\boxed{\text{Form }\Lambda\text{ Fit }\Delta\text{ Function}}
\]
is a typed grammar rather than an untyped equation.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Form} declares the carrier, available operations, invariants, boundaries, and reader relevant to the claim.
\item \textbf{Fit} is the witnessed compatibility relation under which the promised structure may be carried.
\item \textbf{Function} is the operation lawfully carried through admitted Fit.
\item $\Lambda$ marks admitted conjunction or closure.
\item $\Delta$ marks retained distinction; admission does not erase the office of Function.
\end{itemize}

For independently typed constructions
\[
\mathcal K=(X,\mathcal A,\rho,C,\mathcal Q,\mathcal R),
\qquad
\mathcal K'=(X',\mathcal A',\rho',C',\mathcal Q',\mathcal R'),
\]
a map $\phi:X\to X'$ earns transport Fit from $T$ to $T'$ only when the required transport square commutes:
\[
\boxed{\phi\circ T=T'\circ\phi.}
\]
If the readers are also transported by $\bar\phi:Y\to Y'$, readable Fit requires
\[
\boxed{\rho'\circ\phi=\bar\phi\circ\rho.}
\]
If receipts are transported by $\phi_{\mathcal R}$ and occurrence addresses by $\phi_O$, promised lossless carriage requires
\[
\boxed{
\phi_{\mathcal R}(R\frown o)
=
\phi_{\mathcal R}(R)\frown\phi_O(o).
}
\]
A visual or numerical resemblance supplies none of these equations by itself.

\section{Reference, identity, difference, and return}

The reader induces the declared equivalence relation
\[
x\sim_\rho y
\Longleftrightarrow
\rho(x)=\rho(y).
\]
Reader return under $T$ occurs when, for some declared $k>0$,
\[
\rho(T^k x)=\rho(x).
\]
Complete-state return is stronger:
\[
T^k x=x.
\]
These statements are distinct types and are never silently exchanged.

Let $R_n\in\mathcal R$ denote the receipt after $n$ admitted occurrences. An occurrence appends
\[
R_{n+1}=R_n\frown o_{n+1}.
\]
Thus reader return can coexist with retained occurrence:
\[
\boxed{
\rho(T^k x)=\rho(x),
\qquad
R_{n+k}\neq R_n.
}
\]
A reader may close while the occurrence record advances.

\section{Silent read}

Whenever the reader codomain carries subtraction, define the reader difference
\[
\Psi_\rho(T;x)=\rho(Tx)-\rho(x).
\]
Its zero locus is
\[
\Sil_\rho
=
\{(T,x):\Psi_\rho(T;x)=0\}.
\]
A silent read certifies only zero difference at that reader. It does not entail zero transport, complete-state equality, or an empty receipt. In particular, the three statements
\[
\Psi_\rho(T^k;x)=0,
\qquad
T^k x\neq x,
\qquad
R_{n+k}\neq R_n
\]
may hold simultaneously when the declared construction supplies the required witnesses.

\section{Confidence and working trust}

Confidence is demonstrated stability of a fixed reference under declared reuse, perturbation, and transport. It is downstream of successful tests and is never produced by declaration alone.

Working trust is the use-facing permission to proceed without rerunning the entire retained history, conditional on the present receipt, scope, and named falsifier. It neither deletes the receipt nor makes repair unavailable.

Enforced fixation without admissible refusal is imposed closure rather than confidence.

\section{The calculable audit}

Every load-bearing claim is submitted as
\[
\mathfrak A=(D,C,\mathcal Q,T,\rho,I,W,F),
\]
with jurisdiction $D$, condition $C$, constraint $\mathcal Q$, transport $T$, reader $\rho$, invariant $I$, witness $W$, and falsifier $F$.

The audit is executed in order:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Type.} Are every object, operation, equality, and codomain declared?
\item \textbf{Condition.} Does the input satisfy $C$?
\item \textbf{Constraint.} Does the proposed continuation satisfy $\mathcal Q$?
\item \textbf{Transport.} Is $T$ defined on the admitted input?
\item \textbf{Preservation.} Is the required invariant preserved?
\item \textbf{Return.} Does the declared reader close exactly where claimed?
\item \textbf{Receipt.} Is occurrence preserved to the promised resolution without overwrite?
\item \textbf{Witness.} Can the result be recomputed or independently checked?
\item \textbf{Falsifier.} Is there a named observation or calculation that can fail the claim?
\end{enumerate}

The first failed gate stops the present continuation. A later success does not repair an earlier type failure.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Status} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
\midrule
\textsf{CERTIFIED} & all declared gates passed under the stated warrant;\\
\textsf{DERIVED} & follows from certified premises by an admitted derivation;\\
\textsf{DECLARED} & definition, convention, parameter, or scope choice;\\
\textsf{OPEN} & required bridge, proof, estimate, or evidence remains unsupplied;\\
\textsf{REFUSED} & a named gate failed and the failed trace is retained.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

No status is inherited merely because a neighboring office is certified.

\section{Finite matrix witness}

On $V=\mathbb R^2$, let
\[
Q=
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C_1=-\Omega,
\qquad
G=Q\Omega-\Omega Q
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Let
\[
\Phi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2},
\qquad
\Psi=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
Direct multiplication gives
\[
Q^2=Q+I,
\qquad
Q^{\mathsf T}\Omega Q=-\Omega,
\qquad
C_1^2=-I,
\qquad
C_1^4=I,
\qquad
G^2=5I.
\]

\textbf{Proposition 7.1 (Complete local witness).}
The four matrices $\{I,Q,\Omega,G\}$ form a basis of $M_2(\mathbb R)$.

\textit{Proof.}
Using row-major vectorization and the four matrices as columns gives
\[
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0&-2\\
0&1&1&1\\
0&1&-1&1\\
1&0&0&2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det B=-10\neq0.
\]
Hence the four matrices are linearly independent; since $\dim M_2(\mathbb R)=4$, they form a basis. \hfill$\square$

With
\[
H=\frac{G}{\sqrt5},
\]
one has $H^2=I$. The witness therefore contains a quarter-turn generator, a four-step exact phase return, a non-closing Fibonacci transport, an orientation-reversing two-form relation, an involutive mirror, and the complete local real $2\times2$ algebra. These are matrix statements only.

For $n=4c+p$ with $0\le p<4$, define the phase read
\[
r_4(n)=C_1^n=C_1^p.
\]
Then
\[
r_4(n+4)=r_4(n),
\qquad
\left\lfloor\frac{n+4}{4}\right\rfloor
=
\left\lfloor\frac n4\right\rfloor+1.
\]
The phase returns while the occurrence coordinate advances.

\section{Three exact realizations}

\subsection*{Continuous axial lift}

Define
\[
S=\frac{2Q-I}{\sqrt5}.
\]
Since $Q^2=Q+I$,
\[
(2Q-I)^2=5I,
\qquad
S^2=I.
\]
Let
\[
L_t=e^{t(\log\Phi)S}.
\]
The eigenvectors of $Q$ with eigenvalues $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ are also the $+1$ and $-1$ eigenvectors of $S$. Under the M\"obius action of $L_t$ on the upper half-plane, define
\[
\xi_{\mathrm{ax}}(z)=\frac{\Phi-z}{z-\Psi},
\qquad
\Lambda(z)=\log\!\left(\frac{\Phi}{|\xi_{\mathrm{ax}}(z)|}\right),
\qquad
T_0=2\log\Phi.
\]
Projective diagonalization in the two fixed eigendirections gives
\[
\boxed{
\xi_{\mathrm{ax}}(L_tz)=\Phi^{-2t}\xi_{\mathrm{ax}}(z),
\qquad
\Lambda(L_tz)=\Lambda(z)+tT_0.
}
\]
Thus the lifted coordinate advances by a fixed amount under one unit of carriage.

\subsection*{Circle-valued return reader}

Define
\[
\Clock(z)=
\exp\!\left(2\pi i\frac{\Lambda(z)}{T_0}\right).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\Clock(L_tz)=e^{2\pi i t}\Clock(z).
}
\]
Hence every integer carriage step returns the circle-valued reader while $\Lambda$ advances by an integer multiple of $T_0$. Quarter-step sampling yields the four phases $1,i,-1,-i$ without identifying periodic display with lifted position.

\subsection*{Representation-difference calculus}

Let $\varrho:G_0\to GL(W)$ be a group representation and define
\[
\Psi_{\varrho}(g)=\varrho(g)-I_W.
\]
Multiplicativity gives
\[
\boxed{
\Psi_{\varrho}(gh)
=
\Psi_{\varrho}(g)
+
\Psi_{\varrho}(h)
+
\Psi_{\varrho}(g)\Psi_{\varrho}(h).
}
\]
Indeed, expanding the right side yields $\varrho(g)\varrho(h)-I_W$. Also,
\[
\boxed{
\Psi_{\varrho}(g)=0
\Longleftrightarrow
g\in\ker\varrho.
}
\]
Therefore a nonidentity group element may be silent at a declared representation. If an independent receipt retains the occurrence or group address, represented zero does not erase the event.

The three realizations display the same methodological separation in different exact forms: lifted advance versus periodic read, and represented zero versus retained occurrence. They do not identify their carriers.

\section{Scope wall}

This method does not, by itself:
\begin{itemize}
\item identify objects merely because they share a normal form;
\item turn a computational model into an empirical law;
\item transfer certification across an undeclared adapter;
\item erase occurrence because a reader returns zero;
\item certify priority, physical interpretation, or universal scope;
\item replace a missing bridge with confidence, analogy, or authority.
\end{itemize}

It does permit confidence after repeated, reproducible survival under the declared gates.

\section{Canonical statement}

\begin{lawbox}
\textbf{Condition} declares what must hold. \textbf{Constraint} declares what must survive. \textbf{Form} supplies the lawful carrier. \textbf{Fit} supplies the witnessed compatibility relation and, where required, its typed adapter. \textbf{Function} carries the admitted operation. A reader may return while occurrence remains. A zero read does not erase a retained path. Confidence follows demonstrated stability under reuse; working trust carries that warrant forward only inside its declared limit.
\end{lawbox}

Everything further is construction.
